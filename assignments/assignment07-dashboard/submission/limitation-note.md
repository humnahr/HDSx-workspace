# Dashboard Limitation Note

The dashboard uses unweighted descriptive data from the prepared NHANES classroom dataset. It shows differences in average BMI across survey cycles but cannot be used to make conclusions about the US population or determine whether income causes changes in BMI.

Another limitation is that the y-axis does not start at zero, which can make small differences in average BMI seem larger than they actually are. The graph also connects the available NHANES cycles without showing the gaps between certain survey periods, which may give the impression that the data is continuous.
