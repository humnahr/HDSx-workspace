**Figure 1. Mean BMI by income group across NHANES cycles.**

Mean BMI among adults aged 20–80, grouped by income category across the available NHANES cycles using the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset. Records with missing BMI, income group, or cycle values were excluded, along with cycle–income groups containing fewer than 30 observations. The averages are unweighted, so they should not be treated as nationally representative estimates. The graph shows differences between income groups over time but does not establish a causal relationship.
